## What is VCP?

The **Value Creation Protocol (VCP)** is the protocol identity of the Value-First methodology — the methodology expressed in a form that machine systems can parse, declare, and execute without regressing to industrial-age defaults.

VCP is not a new methodology. It is the existing Value-First methodology given a place to stand alongside the protocols that AI-native systems already understand.

### VCP in the Protocol Stack

VCP sits atop two existing protocols and inherits from neither:

- **MCP (Model Context Protocol)** gives AI systems **capability** — the ability to call tools, read context, and act in external systems.
- **HCP (Human Context Protocol)** gives AI systems **human context** — the ability to represent and respect the interests, agency, and lived reality of the humans involved.
- **VCP (Value Creation Protocol)** gives organizations **methodology** — the ability to translate AI capability and human context into realized value for all stakeholders.

The stack reads bottom-up as capability → context → methodology. Each layer is necessary; none is sufficient on its own. An organization with MCP and HCP but no VCP has the substrate for AI-native operations but no operating discipline. An organization with VCP but no MCP/HCP has the operating discipline but no machine-executable substrate.

### Why VCP Exists

Foundation models encode industrial-age B2B vocabulary — leads, funnels, pipeline, conversion, capture — at a depth that prompt engineering and skills cannot reliably override. When AI systems generate content, structure data, or recommend action, they default to that vocabulary because it dominates training data.

VCP exists to give the Value-First methodology a protocol-level identity that AI systems can recognize, declare, and execute with discipline. The methodology stays human-readable. VCP is what makes it machine-parseable.

## What VCP Claims

VCP makes five load-bearing claims that distinguish it from industrial-age operating protocols:

### 1. Mutual Value Creation

Value is created across stakeholders, not extracted from them. Customers, employees, operators, and the organization itself are all participants in value creation. Protocols that frame value as extracted from one party for the benefit of another encode an industrial-age default that AI-native operations should not inherit.

### 2. Context as Substrate

Context is not an input to operations — it is the substrate on which operations run. VCP treats unified context (relationship history, preference signals, prior commitments, current state) as the operating layer that all decisions reference. Fragmented context produces fragmented operations regardless of methodology overlay.

### 3. Configuration Over Customization

VCP favors configuring native platform capability over customizing bespoke architecture. Custom architecture creates consultant dependency and brittle integrations; configuration leverages platform investment and improves with the platform. This claim is platform-agnostic — it applies to any system serving as the operational substrate.

### 4. Relationships Over Transactions

Relationships are the unit of value creation; transactions are events within relationships. Protocols that optimize for transactional outcomes at the expense of relationship integrity produce short-term lift and long-term degradation. VCP-aligned operations optimize for relationship trajectory, with transactions as instruments.

### 5. Just-In-Time Over Just-In-Case

Operations are designed to deliver capability when needed, not to accumulate capability against hypothetical need. Just-In-Case operations produce platform sprawl, integration debt, and operational complexity without proportionate value. Just-In-Time operations require the unified context substrate to function.

## What VCP Does Not Claim

VCP is precise about what it is not, because protocol-stack identity is easy to overstate:

- **VCP is not a competitor to MCP.** It sits atop MCP and depends on it. Implementations that claim VCP without MCP underneath are claiming the methodology layer without the capability substrate.
- **VCP is not a competitor to HCP.** It complements HCP. HCP encodes human context for AI systems; VCP encodes the methodology for translating that context into organizational value.
- **VCP is not a replacement for the Value-First methodology.** It is the methodology's protocol identity. Every existing Value-First framework — Value Path, Three-Org Model, Four Unified Views, 12 Complexity Traps, Five Core Beliefs, Value Loop, Value Realities, Value-Led Growth — remains in force. VCP names and packages the methodology for protocol-stack contexts.
- **VCP is not a marketing repositioning.** It is a technical and methodological identity. The Value-First firm continues to lead with methodology in public-facing materials. VCP shows up where it is load-bearing — technical architecture, AI-native ecosystem positioning, the encoding stack work — and stays out of the way where it is not.
- **VCP is not finished.** The protocol exists; the substrate that makes it executable (the encoding stack) is in active development.

## VCP and the Encoding Stack

VCP requires substrate to be machine-parseable. The substrate is the **three-layer encoding stack**:

- **Layer A — Lexicon (Lexical):** The structured vocabulary of VCP. Each entry encodes a load-bearing term with its industrial counterpart, operational definition, distinguishing signals, and contrastive training pairs. The Lexicon is the formal evolution of the Value-First Language Translation Guide.
- **Layer B — VCP-Lang (Structural):** The grammar for declaring Values, Context, and Trust in parseable syntax. VCP-Lang declarations are the structured statements that AI systems can read, validate, and execute against. VCP-Lang descends from the March 2026 HCP-Lang work.
- **Layer C — Value Graph (Relational):** The specification for encoding mutual value creation as a platform-agnostic relational structure. The Value Graph formalizes the relational frameworks that Value-First methodology already describes — particularly the Three-Org Model and the Value Path — into a graph specification AI systems can traverse.

The encoding stack is not VCP — it is the substrate that makes VCP executable. VCP can be referenced without invoking the encoding stack; the encoding stack should not be referenced without invoking VCP, because the stack exists to serve the protocol.

## Disambiguation: VCP vs. CVP

VCP and CVP share three letters and operate at different layers. Clear separation is required in all materials.

**Value Creation Protocol (VCP)**
- Methodology-as-protocol
- Layer: protocol stack (above MCP and HCP)
- Domain: how organizations declare and execute value creation in machine-parseable form
- Audience: technical architecture, AI-native ecosystem, encoding stack contexts

**Customer Value Platform (CVP)**
- Platform category
- Layer: operational substrate
- Domain: the unified business system on which VCP-aligned operations run
- Current best-fit implementation: HubSpot
- Audience: client-facing implementation, sales conversations, methodology delivery

VCP is what is declared. CVP is what it runs on. They are not synonyms, alternates, or competing names.

## Usage Rules

### When to Use VCP in Content

VCP is used as the lead concept in:

- Technical architecture documentation
- AI-native ecosystem positioning materials
- Protocol-stack content (positioning relative to MCP, HCP)
- Encoding stack documentation (Lexicon, VCP-Lang, Value Graph)
- Developer-facing materials about machine-parseable Value-First

### When Not to Use VCP in Content

VCP is not the lead concept in:

- Public-facing website materials (lead with methodology)
- Sales conversations with mid-market B2B operators (lead with methodology and CVP)
- Client engagement deliverables (lead with methodology)
- The SaaSpocalypse book (lead with methodology)
- The AI-Native Shift cohort (lead with methodology and transformation)
- The AI Native Show (audience is executives, not protocol researchers)

In these contexts, VCP may be referenced when relevant, but should not be the primary frame.

### Naming Conventions

**DO Use:**
- "Value Creation Protocol" or "VCP" (with first reference spelled out)
- "VCP-aligned operations" (when describing operations that conform to the protocol)
- "VCP-Lang" (when referencing the structural layer specifically)
- "The protocol stack: MCP, HCP, VCP" (when establishing position)

**DO NOT Use:**
- "VCP methodology" (the methodology is Value-First; VCP is its protocol identity)
- "VCP platform" (VCP is a protocol, not a platform — that's CVP)
- "Value Capture Protocol" (rejected naming; capture is industrial-age vocabulary)
- "Value-First Protocol" (the firm is Value-First; the protocol is VCP)

### Positioning Language

**DO Say:**
- "VCP gives the Value-First methodology a protocol-stack identity"
- "VCP sits atop MCP and HCP in the AI-native protocol stack"
- "VCP-aligned operations require unified context as substrate"
- "The encoding stack is the substrate that makes VCP machine-parseable"

**DO NOT Say:**
- "VCP replaces the Value-First methodology" (it doesn't — it gives the methodology a protocol identity)
- "VCP is a competitor to MCP" (it's not — it sits atop MCP)
- "VCP is the same as CVP" (they operate at different layers)
- "VCP is a marketing framework" (it's a protocol, not a marketing concept)

## Trigger Conditions for Elevating VCP to Umbrella Positioning

VCP currently operates as the technical and methodological identity of Value-First, not as the firm's umbrella brand position. Elevation to umbrella positioning requires substrate maturity.

The conditions for elevation are:

1. **Lexicon coverage** — Substantial entry coverage across the Value-First vocabulary, not the initial draft batch.
2. **VCP-Lang in production** — At least one client implementation running VCP-Lang declarations in working state.
3. **Value Graph reference implementation** — At least one working Value Graph instance demonstrating the relational substrate.
4. **External validation** — Citation, adoption, or recognition of VCP outside Value-First Team.

When these conditions are met, the elevation is earned. Until then, leading with VCP is asking the market to take it on faith — which is exactly the failure mode VCP exists to prevent.

The decision to elevate is reserved for Chris Carolan as Founder.
