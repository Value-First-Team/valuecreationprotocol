## Purpose

This document establishes the **official language standards** for all Value-First Team communications. This is not stylistic preference—it's foundational methodology. Using industrial-age language contradicts Value-First philosophy at its core.

**The Core Principle:** Language reveals values. Show me how you talk about people, and I'll show you how you treat them.

**When in doubt, check this document.**

---

## Why Language Matters

### Language Shapes Thinking

When we call humans "leads," we:
- Reduce people to objects to be processed
- Reinforce extraction mindset
- Perpetuate asynchronous, conversion-focused thinking
- Signal industrial-age thinking we claim to be replacing

When we use collaborative language, we:
- Honor human agency and progression
- Reinforce partnership mindset
- Enable synchronous, value-focused thinking
- Signal the transformation we represent

### The Prospector Inversion

**Traditional Thinking:** We prospect for leads (we hunt, they're prey)
**Value-First Thinking:** They prospect for solutions; we are the prospects they seek to discover

This inversion changes everything. When we recognize THEY are prospecting and WE are the prospects, we focus on being genuinely valuable, easily discoverable, and immediately helpful—rather than hunting, qualifying, and converting.

## Forbidden Terms (Never Use)

These terms are **absolutely prohibited** in all Value-First content, communications, and internal discussions:

### People Terms
| ❌ Never Use | Why Forbidden |
|-------------|---------------|
| Leads | Reduces humans to objects |
| Prospects (as noun for people) | Implies we hunt them |
| Targets | Implies we attack them |
| MQL / SQL / SAL / PQL | Artificial stages that fight natural behavior |

### Process Terms
| ❌ Never Use | Why Forbidden |
|-------------|---------------|
| Lead generation | Implies manufacturing humans |
| Lead scoring | Reduces humans to numbers |
| Lead nurturing | Implies programmatic manipulation |
| Lead qualification | Gatekeeping language |
| Prospecting (as our activity) | We're not hunters |
| Conversion | Implies manipulation of state |
| Convert | Same as above |
| Closed-won / Closed-lost | Mechanical, transaction-focused |

### Structure Terms
| ❌ Never Use | Why Forbidden |
|-------------|---------------|
| Funnel | Implies forcing people through stages |
| Pipeline stages (for human progression) | Mechanical framing |
| Top of funnel / TOFU | Funnel language |
| Middle of funnel / MOFU | Funnel language |
| Bottom of funnel / BOFU | Funnel language |

### Action Terms
| ❌ Never Use | Why Forbidden |
|-------------|---------------|
| Capture (leads/attention) | Predatory language |
| Target (as verb) | Implies attack |
| Nurture (sequences/campaigns) | Programmatic manipulation |
| Drip campaigns | Mechanical, impersonal |
| Qualify / Disqualify | Gatekeeping |

## The Value Path Stages (Official Language)

When describing human progression, use the eight Value Path stages:

### Path TO Value (Discovery & Evaluation)

| Stage | Mantra | Use When... |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| **Audience** | "I am learning" | Person is casually exploring, building awareness |
| **Researcher** | "I am researching" | Person is actively investigating options |
| **Hand Raiser** | "I need help" | Person has explicitly signaled readiness for guidance |
| **Buyer** | "I am buying" | Person is actively making purchasing decisions |

### Path OF Value (Creation & Multiplication)

| Stage | Mantra | Use When... |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| **Value Creator** | "I must create value" | Person is implementing, proving the decision |
| **Adopter** | "I realize your value" | Person is experiencing genuine value realization |
| **Advocate** | "I tell others about you" | Person is naturally sharing success |
| **Champion** | "I am a raving fan" | Person is co-creating, advancing methodology |

## Context-Specific Applications

### Technical Discussions

The HubSpot "Lead" object exists as technical architecture. In purely technical contexts:

**Acceptable:**
- "The Lead object in HubSpot stores..."
- "Configure the Lead lifecycle stage to..."
- "The Contact-to-Lead association..."

**Required Context:**
- Always clarify this is technical terminology
- Never use "Lead" language in client-facing descriptions of the same functionality
- Consider "(Signal)" notation: "Lead (Signal) lifecycle stage"

### Client Communications

**Never use** forbidden terms in:
- Proposals and scoping documents
- Email communications
- Meeting agendas and notes
- Presentations and decks
- Reports and deliverables

**Always use** Value-First alternatives in all client-facing content.

### Internal Communications

The team should use Value-First language internally to reinforce the methodology. Speaking one way internally and another externally creates cognitive dissonance and eventual drift.

### Marketing and Content

All marketing content, blog posts, social media, and educational materials must use Value-First language exclusively. No exceptions.

## Common Objections and Responses

### "But everyone uses 'lead' terminology"

**Response:** Industry convention doesn't make it right. Everyone once used "Personnel Department" before recognizing "Human Resources" honored humanity better. Language evolves when we recognize better alternatives. Our different language is our differentiator.

### "Our systems are built around leads"

**Response:** Systems serve philosophy; philosophy doesn't serve systems. HubSpot's "Lead" object is technical architecture, not prescriptive language. We use platform capabilities to enable humanized growth.

### "This seems like semantics, not substance"

**Response:** Language shapes thinking. "Lead" terminology perpetuates extraction mindset and mechanical processing patterns. Show me how you talk about people, and I'll show you how you treat them.

### "We'll confuse people with different terminology"

**Response:** Nobody says "I'm an MQL" or "I'm SQL-ready." They DO say "I'm researching" or "I need expert guidance." Our language matches human reality—that's less confusing, not more.

### "How do we measure success without lead metrics?"

**Response:** Lead metrics measure activity and advancement speed, not relationship quality or value creation. We measure what matters: relationship health, progression quality, trust development, collaborative value creation.

## Self-Check Process

Before publishing or sending any content, run this check:

### Scan for Forbidden Terms
Search for: lead, prospect, target, funnel, conversion, convert, closed-won, MQL, SQL, nurture, drip, qualify, capture

### Replace with Alternatives
Use the translation tables above to find appropriate replacements

### Verify Tone
Does the language honor human agency? Does it feel collaborative? Does it match how the person would describe their own experience?

### Final Read
Read aloud. Does it sound like something a person would say to a friend, or something a machine would say to a database entry?

## Governance Process

### When Creating Content

1. **Run the self-check** before finalizing any content
2. **Use find/replace** to catch common forbidden terms
3. **Read aloud** to verify natural, human tone
4. **Ask:** "Would I use this language talking to a friend?"

### When Reviewing Content

1. **Search for forbidden terms** listed in this document
2. **Update to canonical alternatives** when found
3. **Note patterns** that suggest training opportunities
4. **Celebrate** well-executed Value-First language

### When Training New Team Members

1. **Share this document** as foundational reading
2. **Explain the "why"** not just the rules
3. **Practice translation** with example sentences
4. **Provide feedback** on early content with kindness
